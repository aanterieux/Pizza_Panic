# Pizza Panic
The world ended, but the pizza orders still come\
Burn pizzas in the furnace, turn them into crispy ammunition, and survive the horde!

## Game info
**Developer**: Anto Méga\
**Title**: Pizza Panic\
**Version**: 0.5.0\
**Status**: In Development\
**Release date**: TBD\
**Genres**:
- First-person Survival
- Shooter
- Resource Management
- Action Roguelike
	
## Description
- You are a lone pizza delivery guy stuck on at his workplace due to a zombie apocalypse
- The zombies follow you around to try to eat you, you have to make use of various traps and to shoot them with your weapon using burnt pizza crumbs as ammunition
- You have to burn pizzas in the furnace to get ammo
